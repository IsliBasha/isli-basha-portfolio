# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers, recruiters, and potential collaborators landing on the portfolio. They arrive with some technical literacy and a short attention span. They want to quickly gauge whether Isli is someone who can build real things and has taste — not just a list of technologies.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Isli Basha, full-stack developer from Tirana, Albania. The portfolio exists to demonstrate technical range and personality in one surface. Success means a visitor leaves remembering the experience, not just the resume items. The Win95 OS simulation is the core concept: the UI is itself the project.

## Brand Personality

Nostalgic, technical, dry-humored. Confident without performing confidence. "Fast, minimal, useful things."

## Anti-references

- Generic dark-mode dev portfolios with hero sections, glowing tech stack badges, and a scrolling timeline
- AI-generated "clean and modern" layouts that feel interchangeable with a thousand others
- Maximalist creative-agency scroll experiences with heavy animation and no information density
- Anything that looks like it was assembled from a template in an afternoon

## Design Principles

1. **The concept is the content.** The Win95 simulation is not decoration — it is the portfolio. Every design decision should reinforce the OS metaphor, not compete with it.
2. **Show, don't tell.** Minesweeper, Snake, the terminal tree, and the boot sequence demonstrate craft. Avoid listing qualities; let the product embody them.
3. **Minimal surface, maximum signal.** Every element earns its place. Nothing decorative without purpose. When in doubt, remove.
4. **Performance is the argument.** A dev who claims to care about bundle size and render cost should have a site that is visibly fast. The portfolio is the proof of claim.
5. **Dry wit over try-hard quirk.** The humor comes from restraint — an unexpected BSOD, a boot chime, a sticky note — not from shouting personality. Understatement is the register.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific WCAG level mandated. `prefers-reduced-motion` is already implemented; skip link is present. Maintain what is there and do not regress it. AA contrast as a practical floor.
